Mic Optimizer is installed.

BEFORE YOU START
Mic Optimizer sends your processed microphone to a virtual audio device called VB-Cable. If you have not installed it yet, get it free from https://vb-audio.com/Cable/ (restart Windows if the installer asks). The app shows a banner with a download button when VB-Cable is missing.

HOW TO USE IT
1. Open Mic Optimizer from the tray icon (near the clock; on Windows 11 it may be under the ^ arrow, drag it out to keep it visible).
2. If you are not sure which input your microphone is on, press "Find my mic" and speak for 3 seconds.
3. In Discord, OBS, Teams or your recorder, choose "CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)" as the microphone.
4. Turn off the Direct Monitor button on your audio interface if you want to hear only the processed sound in your headphones.

Closing the window keeps Mic Optimizer running in the tray. Use the tray icon's menu, Exit, to quit it completely.

Your profiles and settings are stored in %APPDATA%\MicOptimizer and are kept when you uninstall.
